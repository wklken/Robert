## Contributing

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the repository's
[development guide](docs/development.md). The current beta does not require DCO sign-off or a CLA.

## Code of Conduct

Participation is governed by [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), including its reporting process.

## Security

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Do not open public issues containing exploit details or credentials.

## Support

Choose a channel and prepare a minimal, redacted report using [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
